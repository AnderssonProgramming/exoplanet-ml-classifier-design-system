Problem & motivation

Kepler produced ~10,000 KOIs.
Only some of them are planets.

  • ~150,000 stars observed by the Kepler primary mission, generating periodic dimming events.Eclipsing binaries, instrument noise, and stellar variability mimic real transits.
  • Manual vetting is slow — automating the triage step accelerates follow-up campaigns.NASA assigns CONFIRMED / CANDIDATE / FALSE POSITIVE; we focus on the binary triage decision.
  • Decision supported: Should this KOI be promoted to a follow-up observation queue?
